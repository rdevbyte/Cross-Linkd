Two ways to use the directory

How CrossLinkd works

Discover community-listed businesses and organizations, or create a free listing for your own business.

Listing on CrossLinkd does not constitute an endorsement. Please independently evaluate each business, church, ministry, or organization before engaging with it.

The path, in five steps

A completeness review checks whether the form is filled in. It does not verify credentials.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Submit or discover a listing

    Owners and community members can submit a profile. Visitors can search what is already published.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Profile reviewed for completeness

    CrossLinkd may check for missing fields and obvious inconsistencies. That is not a check of licenses, credentials, faith, or quality.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    Listing published

    A published profile is owner-submitted. Publishing is not an endorsement, and an incomplete submission is not published.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Owner claims or confirms the profile

    Owner claimed means someone showed control of a contact channel. Recently confirmed by owner means the owner said the details are current.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Listing is maintained or reported by the community

    Owners can update a profile. Readers can report a concern. A report is not an investigation.

Status details describe how this profile was submitted or updated. They do not guarantee business quality, licensing, pricing, outcomes, Christian ownership, or theological agreement.

For business owners

Free forever for the basic listing. No credit card required.

  1. 1

    Submit a basic listing

    Name, type, and contact details. The basic listing is free. No credit card, and no identity documents.

  2. 2

    Provide accurate information

    You are responsible for the text, including any faith or credential statement.

  3. 3

    Receive a completeness review

    Missing fields may be flagged. This does not confirm Christian ownership, a license, or service quality.

  4. 4

    Claim or confirm the listing

    Show control of a contact channel. That status is Owner claimed, not a verification.

  5. 5

    Add more information

    Hours, service area, and a faith statement are optional. They stay self-reported.

  6. 6

    Keep the profile current

    Edit claimed listings from the dashboard when details change.

  7. 7

    Respond to corrections or reports

    Readers can flag a problem. You can correct the profile or reply through the contact form.

Required. Listing type, name, and this attestation: “I confirm that the information submitted is accurate to the best of my knowledge.” No identity documents, licenses, or private religious records are requested.
Public. Only contact fields you mark as public appear on the profile. A faith statement is optional and self-reported.
After submit. A complete form may publish as owner-submitted. That is not guaranteed if required fields are missing, and it is not an endorsement. Update a claimed listing in the dashboard, or request removal.
